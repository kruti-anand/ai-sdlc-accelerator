DESIGN PRINCIPLE

AI accelerates the work.
People own the decisions.

The accelerator is designed to increase delivery speed and clarity without replacing Product, Architecture, Engineering, QA, Security, or other accountable decision makers.

AI

AI can structure ambiguous input, identify gaps, surface dependencies, draft artifacts, analyze existing information, and accelerate preparation.

H

Product and engineering stakeholders review assumptions, requirements, architecture, risks, security considerations, and implementation decisions before work moves forward.
